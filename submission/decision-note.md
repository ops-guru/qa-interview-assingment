# Decision note

At most 600 words for the five sections below. Bullets and compact tables are sufficient.

## 1. Two priority findings

For each: observation → hypothesis or risk → next evidence.

## 2. Delivery sequence

First two weeks; day 30; day 60; day 90. Include ownership and one deferral.

## 3. Quality approach

Test layers, regression time, gates/environments, and shared versus squad responsibilities.

## 4. Performance and client decision

Missing workload information, proposed acceptance criteria, next experiment, and a two-sentence message to the client VP.

## 5. Repeatable offering

Client need, scope/boundaries, reusable assets, and a business outcome to measure.

---

The following sections are outside the word limit.

## Run instructions

Runtime/dependencies, test command, spike command, and where results appear.

## Time and unfinished work

Time spent, what remains, and next steps.

## AI disclosure

Tools used (or none); where they helped; what you changed or validated; remaining assumptions or limitations.
